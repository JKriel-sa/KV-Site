How a film works.

  1. Brief

    What the film is for, who's in it, and where it'll play. That decides length, format and how we shoot.

  2. Plan

    Shot list, locations, interview questions and a call sheet, so the day is spent filming, not deciding.

  3. Shoot

    Directed, filmed and recorded in house. Gimbal, lighting and audio sized to the job.

  4. Cut and deliver

    A first cut, one round of notes built in, then the master and every cutdown you need, graded and subtitled.
