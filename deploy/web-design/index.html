How a site gets built.

  1. Brief

    What the site needs to do, roughly how big it is, and what already exists. The estimator asks this in plain language.

  2. Design

    Built up from your photography. If you don't have it yet, we shoot it. That's rather the point of the studio.

  3. Build

    Hand-coded, no template, no plugin sprawl. Fast on a phone, easy for us to change later.

  4. Launch and care

    We put it live, connect the domain and analytics, then stay on to keep it current.
