How a shoot works.

  1. Brief

    What, where, how many images, and where they'll be used. The estimator asks exactly this, so by the time we talk the shape is already clear.

  2. Shoot

    On location or in studio, with a shot list we agreed beforehand and room to chase the picture that wasn't on it.

  3. Select

    A proof gallery you choose from. We suggest; you decide.

  4. Deliver

    Finished, retouched files in the sizes you need, licensed for the uses you told us about.
