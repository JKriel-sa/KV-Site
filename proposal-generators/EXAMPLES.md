# Worked examples

Three per service. Every figure below came out of the live generator, not off
the back of an envelope — the same code the site runs.

Prices are the **Standard** tier. Good is 15% below it with reduced scope;
Premium is 30% above with additions. All assume a comfortable deadline, so none
carry a rush fee.

## Two things these examples expose

**Licensing can outgrow production.** On the short film the licence comes to
$33,031 against a $29,675 subtotal — cinema, in perpetuity, bought outright,
multiplies to 2.16×. Full buyouts genuinely do cost multiples of the production,
so this is not a bug, but it is the number most worth testing against a real
quote you have won or lost. Both the commercial and the film flag for a human
before anything goes out.

**The spread is wide and that is the point.** A headshot session and a
scored short film are both "audio-visual work" and differ by a factor of fifty.
Anyone quoting those from a single day rate is wrong in one direction or the
other.

## Rates are still placeholders

These examples are internally consistent and the arithmetic is verified, but
the underlying rates are invented. Run three or four past jobs through the
generator where you already know what you invoiced, and move the constants at
the top of each `pg-<service>.js` until the outputs match. Until then, treat
every figure here as a shape rather than a price.
